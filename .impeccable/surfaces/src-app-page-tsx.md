Scope: the landing route `/` (the 3D room and hallway). Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and job: visitors to a personal-project brand who wander, read, listen. Action: reach a section (shelf label, record) or keep walking into the hallway.

Constraints: content from `src/config/site.ts`; no autoplay; 60fps mid-range laptop; plain-links index; reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a room you walk into, not a page you read. It refuses the portfolio grid-of-thumbnails and the full-screen hero with a scroll cue; navigation is furniture.

OWN-WORLD: Inked toon interior. Sage-painted panelling, ochre limewash plaster, oxblood bookcloth spines, a green-black door frame, brass hardware; every edge carries a soft dark ink outline; flat cel bands of light from the window. Hallway: desaturated blue-grey, cream relief wallpaper with L-system plants, flickering two-shade lamps, checker floor fading to fog. UI chrome is a few lines of set text in Bricolage Grotesque, no boxes, no glass. Ink line, shadow hatching and the warm-to-cold grade are one screen pass over the whole scene.

STORY: The visitor understands this is someone's reading room, believes the work is kept here with care, and either takes a book or a record, or keeps going and finds the archive hallway.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Standing at the doorway end of the room, eye height, the bright window centred and small on the far wall, shelf and record player as silhouettes either side. Brand name top-left as set text, "Index" top-right, a diegetic step counter bottom-right ("12 steps to the window"). No headline.

FORM: Brief-pinned first-person room; ranked first on the owner's own list. seed: none (roll skipped: the owner's brief pins the direction, which outranks the roll)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
